## Overview

Give each page a clear title. Use explicit publication and update dates when they are meaningful, and keep navigation order on the immediate parent page.

## Branches and leaves

Branch pages use _index.md and may contain both a body and children. Leaf bundles use index.md and represent the final page at that point in the tree.
