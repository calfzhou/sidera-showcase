## Overview

Work through the outline one section at a time. Prefer a concrete example over a broad claim, and leave a visible question where a detail still needs checking.

## Keep the next step small

Read the draft once for missing ideas. Save sentence-level polishing for a separate pass so that editing does not interrupt the initial explanation.
