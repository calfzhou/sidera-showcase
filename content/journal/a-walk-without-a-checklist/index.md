## Leave room for an interruption

A walk is different when it does not need to produce a result. A shop has changed its sign; a familiar corner sounds quieter; someone has planted flowers beside a bus stop. None of these observations needs to become a project.

## Keep one detail

Write down the detail that remained after returning home. One concrete sentence is often more useful than a complete account of the route. A notebook can hold an unfinished thought without asking for an explanation.
