## Return to a question

Begin a review with something you want to understand or do. Search for the notes that help with that question, then repair the parts that prevented them from being useful.

## Stop before polishing becomes the goal

An old note can remain rough if it still carries a good observation. Correct an inaccurate statement, add a missing source or explain a link; do not rewrite a whole page only because its wording is no longer how you would begin today.

Making notes ends here, but the notebook does not. A small beginning, repeated visits, useful connections and selective review form a routine that can continue without a fixed number of entries.
